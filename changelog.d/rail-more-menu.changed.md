The web terminal's rail is quieter: Docs and Feedback stay at its foot, and a
new **More** menu holds SYSTEM, the tour and the activity log. SYSTEM opens
from More or from the status bar's health dot without taking a rail entry, and
its tile header offers **Pin to rail**. Docs and Feedback can be hidden from
their own right-click menu and come back from More. The control-assistant
presets keep SYSTEM enabled but off the rail (`web.panels.system-health.hidden`).
